🌀 Arisan on Tari Ootle

A rotating savings circle, the way millions of people in Indonesia save together — with no organiser holding the pot. Everyone puts in the same amount each round; each round, one member takes the whole pot home, in join order. If someone stops paying, the circle expels them, refunds everyone who paid, and carries on.

⚠️ DEMO MODE — simulation. This page runs the same rules as the ArisanCircle Rust template in your browser, with play money. No wallet, no funds, no network. The template itself is tested against the real Ootle engine (cargo test in the repo). On-chain, members are signer public keys, not names.

Guided walkthrough — what to try (≈ 3 minutes)

  1. Create a circle (10 TARI, max 4 members) and add Budi, Citra & Dewi.
  2. Start the circle. Notice the round deadline: contributions are due by then.
  3. Try paying the wrong amount — the template rejects it and charges nothing.
  4. Everyone pays round 0, then pay out the pot: Ana (member #0) receives 40 TARI.
  5. Round 1: let everyone pay except Dewi, advance to the deadline, settle the default — Dewi is expelled, the payers are refunded in full, the circle continues with 3.
  6. Finish the remaining rounds. Budi and Citra end whole; Ana keeps Dewi's round-0 contribution (Dewi paid in, then defaulted before her turn) — the loss falls on the defaulter, not the group.
